01 · WEBSITE

A new adidaschampionscup.com

The site was rebuilt for 2027 and is live now at adidaschampionscup.com: built for phones, faster, open to Google and focused on the one thing that matters, getting teams to register.

What changed

  • Live and designed for phones first: most parents and coaches browse on mobile.
  • One venue, one clear message: the Tournament Sportsplex of Tampa Bay.
  • 2027 rules in an easy-to-read accordion; outdated 2026 information removed.
  • "Register your team" buttons in every section, straight to GotSport.
  • Divisions aligned with GotSport: U10–U12 (7v7 and 9v9) and U13–U19 (11v11).
  • 11 old pages redirected (301) to the new home and hidden from search engines.
  • Open to Google, in US English, with Search Console connected and the sitemap submitted.
  • Share preview ready: the link shows the 2027 image (1200x630) on WhatsApp and social media.
  • Google Analytics 4 connected, including every click on a "Register" button.
  • Hotel section hidden until the 2027 hotel information is available.

Sample emails

Why this format

  • Past tournament emails show that repeating the same flyer stops working; new information ("schedule is out", "clubs confirmed") gets the most clicks.
  • One clear button per email, repeated at the top and bottom, straight to GotSport.
  • Short SMS (one segment) with a trackable link, only as reminders.
  • Every link is tagged, so each email and text can be measured on its own.
04 · MEASUREMENT & NEXT STEPS

What we will track

Website

Google Analytics 4: visits, traffic sources and every click on "Register" (register_click, by button). Google Search Console: searches and clicks from Google.

Email + SMS

Opens and clicks per send; unsubscribes and bounces to keep the list clean.

Instagram

Reach, saves, shares and new followers per post; tags from clubs and families.

Registrations

Weekly count of registered teams by division, shared by the organization.

What works

Each month, more of what brings registrations and less of what does not.

Weekly update

A short summary of all channels for the partners.
